One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026 — a 2×2 collage of the day's four most consequential stories, across regions and fields:

1. **Shenzhen summit** — Presidents Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin hold their first three-way meeting in Shenzhen and sign a joint peace declaration.
2. **World Court ruling** — The International Court of Justice rules that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the first such finding against a state by that court.
3. **AI sell-off** — Global stocks plunge on doubts over AI valuations: the Nasdaq wipes out its 2026 gains and Anthropic shares sink 13% the day after its record Nasdaq debut.
4. **Riyadh hit** — Houthi missiles strike Riyadh's financial district, damaging the Saudi sovereign wealth fund (PIF) tower.

`main.py` copies the image `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`), deterministically.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
